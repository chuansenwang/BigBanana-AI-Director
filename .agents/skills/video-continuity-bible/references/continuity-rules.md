# Continuity Rules

## Core principle

Separate **what must stay fixed** from **what is allowed to vary by shot**.

If a fact helps preserve identity across multiple shots, it belongs in the continuity bible.
If a fact only describes one action beat, it belongs in the downstream shot prompt layer.

## Put these in the bible

- stable hairstyle, clothing, body type, silhouette, recurring accessories
- recurring location design and atmosphere
- recurring props and VFX look
- global tone and rendering style
- strong character energy or behavioral signature

## Do not overfit these into the bible

- exact one-shot gestures
- one-time camera moves
- line-by-line acting beats unless they define character energy
- shot-specific composition details that do not affect continuity

## Good bible entries

- "purple wavy bob haircut"
- "oversized comedic pizza with exaggerated cheese pull"
- "warm cozy pizza booth lighting"
- "playful social short video pacing"

## Bad bible entries

- "turns left in shot 03"
- "points to the ground in shot 01"
- "camera crash zooms during transformation"

## Handling uncertainty

When uncertain:

1. keep the stable part
2. remove decorative guesswork
3. add a warning

## Recommended output quality bar

The bible should be reusable across all shots of the same short video without re-describing the world from scratch each time.
