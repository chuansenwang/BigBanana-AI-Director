# T0-T3 Rubric

## T0 决定上限

Focus on the core concept and whether the video has a compelling high-level package.

Check:

- hook premise
- audience fit
- replay / share potential
- novelty or emotional pull

Evidence can come from metadata, opening beat, transcript, and visible payoff structure.

## T1 硬指标

Focus on likely retention drivers and structural mechanics.

Check:

- 前三秒内容
- 节奏推进
- 镜头/段落密度
- 反转或 payoff
- CTA / resolution presence

## T2 软指标

Focus on execution quality.

Check:

- 画风 / 视觉吸引力
- 音乐 / 音效
- 表情与肢体
- 转场与剪辑
- 清晰度与画质

## T3 灵活指标

Focus on contextual upside and platform fit.

Check:

- 发布时间 / 平台适配
- 圈层传播性
- 是否适合作为对标样本
- 特定 niche 的放大因子

## Scoring rule

Use bounded descriptive scoring, not benchmark calibration. Explain why a score is high/medium/low and cite evidence. If transcript or segmentation quality is weak, downgrade confidence before widening claims.
